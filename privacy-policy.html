Privacy Policy

SmartMoneyTrek (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies and third-party advertising as described in this policy.

We may share limited data with service providers (hosting, analytics, ad platforms). We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Your choices

You can opt out of personalized ads in your ad provider settings, disable cookies in your browser, or contact us to request data removal.

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data

You can exercise these rights by contacting us via our Contact Page .

We use reasonable security measures but cannot guarantee absolute safety. If you suspect a breach, please contact us.

Our site is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes.

11. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Contact

You can contact us via our Contact Page for any privacy-related requests, including data access, correction, or deletion.
